Privacy Policy

Smart Calculator: Scientific · Android app

Effective date: 24 November 2026  |  Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

"Smart Calculator: Scientific" (the "App") is published by Bahar Uddin Raihan ("we", "us", "our"), an individual developer. This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Contact: dev.raihan.tech@gmail.com

In short: the App has no accounts, no sign-up and no servers of ours. Your calculations stay on your phone. The only third-party service that receives data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the App free.

2. Information that stays on your device

The App stores the following only on your phone. We never receive it.

  • Calculation history – up to your last 50 calculations, so you can look at them again.
  • A date marker – the day the last full-screen ad was shown, so a full-screen ad appears at most once per day.

You can delete history inside the App at any time (History → Clear, or swipe an item away). Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes all of it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not collect your precise location, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not run our own analytics, tracking or crash-reporting service.
  • The App's own code needs only the standard Internet permission, to load ads. The Google advertising library bundled in the App adds a few more technical permissions to the App's listing: network state (to know whether you are online), advertising ID and the Android Ad Services permissions (used for advertising and ad measurement, see section 4), and wake lock / foreground service (used internally to schedule ad-library background work). None of these gives us access to your contacts, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertising. It shows one banner ad and, at most once per day, a full-screen (interstitial) ad, served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

To deliver, measure and secure ads, Google may collect and use information from your device, which can include:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your device);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software details (model, operating system, language, screen size, network type);
  • app details (app name, version) and how you interact with ads (views, clicks);
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ad SDK;
  • signals used for interest-based advertising and ad measurement, including those provided by Android's Ad Services (such as the Topics and Attribution APIs) on supported devices.

Google acts as an independent controller / business for this data under its own policies. Please read:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your ad choices

  • Personalised ads: on Android you can turn off ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (menu names vary by phone), or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent messages: where the law requires it (for example in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and some US states), the App shows a consent / privacy-choices message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised advertising is used. You can change your answer later from the same message when it is offered in the App.

We have no control over, and receive no personal data from, the ads you are shown. Clicking an ad takes you to a third party whose own privacy policy then applies.

Where UK or EU data-protection law applies, ad-related processing relies on your consent (for personalised ads and device-information access) and on the legitimate interests of Google and us in operating, measuring and securing ad delivery (for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and basic measurement). You may withdraw consent at any time as described in section 4.

7. Your rights

UK and EEA residents may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to their data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

US residents (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and other states with privacy laws) may have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information. Sharing of advertising identifiers with Google AdMob may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws; you can opt out through the privacy-choices message in the App (where shown) or your device ad settings described in section 4. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests are fulfilled by deleting the App's data on your phone. For data held by Google, use the links in section 4. You can also email us at dev.raihan.tech@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time (and within the period required by applicable law).

8. Children

The App is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help; you can also remove the App's data from the device at any time.

9. Data retention and security

History and the date marker remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. We rely on your device's built-in app sandboxing to protect that data. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept under Google's own retention policies.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

Google may process ad-related data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Google uses recognised safeguards for such transfers; see Google's privacy policy for details. We do not transfer your data ourselves because we do not collect it.

11. Changes to this policy

The "Last updated" date above will change, and for significant changes we will take reasonable steps to notify you (such as a note in the App or on the store page). Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy.
